How ordering works

Ordering, delivery & returns.

The key details for placing an order with OneNation S.V.G. We confirm each order directly with the customer before production begins.

How do I place an order?

Contact us by phone or WhatsApp, or through our social channels. Tell us the design, garment size and colour, quantity and any customisation you would like. We will confirm availability, specifications and the final price directly with you. An enquiry is not a confirmed order.

Is a down payment required?

Yes. A down payment is required for every order before production starts. The amount or percentage is agreed individually and confirmed with you before production begins. Any remaining balance and its due date are also agreed for your order.

How can I pay?

Payment can be made through Pay a Peer. Cash is also accepted on collection. The payment method and any remaining-balance arrangements will be confirmed when your order is agreed.

Do you deliver in Kingstown?

Yes. Delivery to places within Kingstown, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is free. We will agree the delivery location and timing directly with you.

Can I collect my order?

Yes. Collection is by appointment from House of Graphics, Richmond Hill Road, Beachmont, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. Please arrange your collection date and time with us before visiting.

Do you deliver outside Kingstown?

Delivery outside Kingstown is not a standard advertised service. Contact us to discuss whether an arrangement may be possible for your location.

What is your return period?

Local customers may request a return within 3 days. Non-local customers may request a return within 30 days. Contact OneNation S.V.G. directly to start a return request.

Can I get a refund, exchange or store credit?

The appropriate resolution—refund, exchange or store credit—is considered individually according to the circumstances. Responsibility for any return-delivery costs is also agreed case by case. Please contact us before sending or bringing an item back.

What if my product is damaged?

Products damaged after delivery or collection through customer handling, misuse, negligence or improper care cannot be returned or exchanged. Please inspect your order when you receive or collect it. If it appears to have arrived damaged or defective, contact us promptly so we can review the situation individually.

How do I contact you?

Call +1 784 492 6777 or message us on WhatsApp. You can also visit our Contact page.
