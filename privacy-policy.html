Privacy Policy

This policy explains how OneNation S.V.G. handles information when you visit our website or contact us about our designs and apparel.

1. Who we are

OneNation S.V.G. is a Caribbean-rooted streetwear and design brand inspired by Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. This website presents our brand, designs and collections and provides ways to contact us.

2. Information you provide

This website does not currently provide customer accounts, an online checkout, or a website payment form. If you contact us by telephone, WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook, you may provide your name, contact details, garment size, order preferences, design ideas and message contents. Please do not send payment-card details or other sensitive information through social messages.

3. Information collected when you visit

Our website uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-LN1ZL3CN25) and a Metricool tracking script to understand website use and improve the experience. These services may process technical and usage information such as pages visited, interactions, browser and device information, approximate location derived from network information, referral information and identifiers stored through cookies or similar technologies. The providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Cookies and advertising

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for analytics, site functionality and measurement. If this site is approved for and begins displaying Google advertising, Google and other advertising partners may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a visitor's prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve personalized ads. Visitors can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings or learn about additional choices at aboutads.info. We will update this policy if our advertising arrangements materially change.

5. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss design and printing requirements, arrange customer orders, maintain and improve the website, understand which pages are useful, protect the site and meet applicable legal obligations.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support website analytics, social messaging or advertising, and may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect rights and security. When you follow a link to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or another external service, that service's own privacy policy and settings apply. We do not control those services.

7. Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage an order or maintain appropriate business records. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide information, adjust cookie settings in your browser, use the advertising controls linked above, or contact us to ask about personal information you have shared with us. Rights and available choices may depend on the laws that apply where you live.

9. Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information through one of our contact channels.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published here with its update date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, call or WhatsApp +1 784 492 6777, or use our Contact page.
